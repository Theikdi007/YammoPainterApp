Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

Panda Paint ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Panda Paint mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign up for Panda Paint, we collect:

  • Email address (when creating an account with email/password)
  • Name and profile picture (when signing in with Google)
  • Google account ID (for Google Sign-In authentication)

Drawings and Images

We store the following data you create within the app:

  • Saved drawings (stored on our servers)
  • AR tracing images you upload

Device Information

We automatically collect:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • App version number

Camera and Photos

Panda Paint accesses your camera and photo library for the following purposes:

  • Camera: Used for AR Drawing feature to overlay tracing images on your camera view
  • Photo Library: Used to save your drawings and to import images as coloring templates
Important: We do NOT access your camera or photos in the background. Access is only requested when you explicitly use these features within the app.

Notifications

We use push notifications to:

  • Inform you about new content or features
  • Send reminders (if enabled)

You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Panda Paint service
  • Save and sync your drawings across devices
  • Authenticate your account
  • Send you push notifications (with your consent)
  • Improve and optimize the app
  • Respond to your support requests

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase cloud infrastructure. We implement appropriate security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (TLS/SSL)
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security audits

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following cases:

  • Service Providers: We use third-party services that may collect information (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase)
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights

5. Third-Party Services

Panda Paint uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Backend-as-a-Service for authentication and data storage — Privacy Policy
  • Firebase: Push notifications and analytics — Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In: Authentication — Privacy Policy

6. Children's Privacy

Panda Paint is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete such information.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of push notifications
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide you with the service. When you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
